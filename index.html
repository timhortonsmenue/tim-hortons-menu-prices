Tim Hortons Cup Sizes Explained

Prices on the menu board only make sense when you know how much drink you get. In Canada, Tim Hortons hot drinks come in these approximate cup sizes:

Smallabout 10 oz · 296 ml
Mediumabout 14 oz · 414 ml
Largeabout 20 oz · 591 ml
Extra Largeabout 24 oz · 710 ml

Extra Large is offered for brewed coffee and tea at many restaurants, but not for every drink, which is why it isn't listed for each item on this board. Going from a Small to a Medium Original Blend adds about four ounces for 24 cents, one of the best upgrades on the Tim Horton menu.

Tims Ordering Lingo

Double-Double
Coffee with two creams and two sugars. The most famous order in Canada.
Triple-Triple
Three creams and three sugars, for a sweeter, creamier cup.
Regular
In much of Canada, one cream and one sugar. Ask if you're not sure what your restaurant means.
Black
Coffee with nothing added.
Timbits
Bite-sized donut holes, sold by the piece or in boxes of 10, 20 and 50.
Iced Capp
Short for iced cappuccino: Tim Hortons' frozen blended coffee drink.
Take Twelve
A carry-out box of coffee that serves twelve cups.
Steeped Tea
Tim Hortons' house black tea, brewed in big batches and often ordered as a "steeped tea, double-double".

Canada's Favourite Tim Hortons Items

Tim Hortons named the Apple Fritter, Chocolate Glazed Timbits and the Double-Double among Canada's favourite menu items of 2025. On today's board, an Apple Fritter is about $1.59, a Chocolate Glazed Timbit about 35 cents and a medium Double-Double about $1.83.

Reading the Board Like a Regular

  • Look at the Medium price first. It is the most ordered size and the best value per ounce for most drinks.
  • Boxes beat singles. A dozen donuts at $14.49 works out to about $1.21 each, and a 50-pack of Timbits to about 20 cents each.
  • Flavoured drinks cost more. A flavoured Iced Capp or latte is usually 30 to 70 cents more than the original.
  • Prices change by location. Airports, campuses and highway stops can charge more than a neighbourhood restaurant, and sales tax is added at the till.
  • Check the app. The Tim Hortons app shows the exact price at your restaurant and lets you order ahead.

Tim Horton Menu Questions

Who owns Tim Hortons?

Tim Hortons is owned by Restaurant Brands International (RBI), which also owns Burger King, Popeyes and Firehouse Subs. Most Tim Hortons restaurants are run by independent franchise owners.

When was Tim Hortons founded?

Tim Hortons was founded in 1964 in Hamilton, Ontario, by hockey player Tim Horton and Jim Charade, and later grew with business partner Ron Joyce.

What are the most popular items on the Tim Hortons menu?

Tim Hortons named the Apple Fritter, Chocolate Glazed Timbits and the Double-Double among Canada's favourite menu items of 2025. Iced Capps and breakfast sandwiches are also top sellers.

What is the cheapest drink at Tim Hortons?

A Small Original Blend or Dark Roast coffee is the cheapest drink at about $1.59. A Small Steeped Tea is about $1.79.

What is the most expensive item on the Tim Hortons menu?

Among single items, Loaded Bowls are the priciest at about $9.99. For groups, the Take Twelve coffee box costs about $19.99 and a dozen muffins about $18.99.

What sizes do Tim Hortons drinks come in?

Most hot and cold drinks come in Small, Medium and Large. A few items, such as some energy drinks and seasonal flavours, come in only one or two sizes.

Does Tim Hortons have vegetarian options?

Yes. Veggie Loaded Wraps and Bowls, bagels with cream cheese, muffins, donuts, Timbits and many drinks have no meat. Check the Tim Hortons app for full ingredient details.

Is this the official Tim Hortons menu?

No. This is an independent fan guide with typical Canadian prices. For official prices and nutrition, use the Tim Hortons app or website.
